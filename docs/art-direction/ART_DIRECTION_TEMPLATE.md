# ART DIRECTION

These templates define the project’s visual world before implementation. They are not moodboards. Approved art direction and A1 assets become production requirements.

## Visual Thesis

Complete:

“We will express [brand/project quality] through [visual principle] so the experience feels [specific result], while preserving [functional requirement].”

## Desired Perception

- What should users notice first?
- What should they feel?
- What should they remember?
- What should never feel generic?
