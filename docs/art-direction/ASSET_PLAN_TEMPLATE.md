# ASSET PLAN

These templates define the project’s visual world before implementation. They are not moodboards. Approved art direction and A1 assets become production requirements.

## Asset Rule

No major production surface should depend on an unapproved placeholder asset.

## Major Asset Brief

For every A1 asset record:

- Purpose
- Where it appears
- What it must communicate
- Subject/content
- Composition
- Lighting
- Palette
- Texture/materiality
- Motion requirements
- Crop requirements
- Mobile variation
- Accessibility requirement
- Performance implications
- Source/licensing
- Approval criteria

## Asset Quality Gate

An A1 asset is not approved merely because it fits the layout.

It must:

- perform its intended role;
- match the approved art direction;
- survive intended crops;
- have sufficient technical resolution/quality;
- avoid obvious generic stock or generic AI characteristics unless intentionally desired;
- work responsively or have an alternate asset;
- have valid rights/licensing for production;
- meet accessibility/media requirements where applicable.

## Missing Asset Rule

If an A1 asset does not exist, mark it as missing.

Do not silently replace it with generic filler simply to continue layout production.
